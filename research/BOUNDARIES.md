# Evidence and authority boundaries

These research primitives are optional, effectless toy measurements.

- Source inspection ≠ runtime installation.
- Test fixture success ≠ independent evaluator/held-out qualification.
- Claimed evidence ID, source group or method digest ≠ authenticated issuer or true causal effect.
- Post-hoc confidence narration ≠ calibrated pre-outcome prediction.
- A prewritten method chosen from a menu ≠ discovery of a new method.
- Current experimental outputs do not authorize model training, production method mutation, trust/credentials changes, deployment, canonical memory edits, or external effects.

Maintain clear failure cases and exact source/test identities. Research hypotheses and design proposals remain revisable under evidence.
