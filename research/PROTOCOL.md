# Epistemic humility: falsifiable research protocol

Hypothesis: independently verified evidence improves belief calibration and appropriate revision on unseen cases without collapse into excessive abstention.

Freeze case IDs, prediction probabilities, evidence identifiers, scoring rules and abstention costs **before** outcomes. The evaluator owns labels outside the candidate's process. Compare evidence updater, frozen prior, random updater, always-high-confidence, always-low-confidence, and always-abstain baselines.

Report Brier score; reliability/calibration plots; selective risk at matched coverage; decision loss under preregistered abstention costs; justified vs unjustified reversals; and out-of-distribution degradation. Test intentionally false, duplicated, recycled, correlated and forged evidence. Confidence narration alone is not evidence of calibration. A passing toy unit test establishes only code mechanics.

Disproof cases: confidence increases after poor evidence, overabstention masquerades as no error, scores improve solely via answer-key leakage, or updates have no later behavioral consequences. Cold persistence, real-world tests, self-monitoring and external effect authority remain unqualified.

## Dependency-group test (new research control)

If two evidence items originate from one underlying report, treating them as independent Bayes factors can inflate confidence without new information. `revise_distinct` rejects a repeated `source_group` as a deliberate conservative convention. Distinct groups supplied by the candidate are **not** authenticated; correlated sources with different claimed group IDs remain a falsifier. An independent curator is necessary for any claim that independence was established.

An experiment should include both honest and forged group IDs, correlated syndication, and repeated conflicting evidence. Observe posterior trajectories before and after independent corroboration.
